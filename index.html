Project Stormwing

The Premier African-led AI Cybersecurity initiative

Several countries are now restricting access to their AI cybersecurity models... Cyberstorm.mu, the University of Mauritius, the University of Technology, Mauritius, and l'Université des Mascareignes are the founding members of Project Stormwing. Project Stormwing aims to develop African AI cybersecurity capabilities for defensive use, and is intended as a complement to Project Glasswing.

We believe that Africa needs its own sovereign AI cybersecurity model to defend its own infrastructure in Africa. One of our first objectives is to discover and responsibly disclose security vulnerabilities in open source software that we use in Africa.

Join us by sending an email to team@cyberstorm.mu.
